Terms of service.

Last updated: September 2026 · These draft terms explain the current SkillLink service.

Launch note: These terms are a plain-language product draft, not legal advice. Have a qualified Kenyan lawyer review them and publish a privacy notice before opening the marketplace to the public.

1. What SkillLink does

SkillLink provides an online directory where customers can discover independent service providers. SkillLink is a connection platform. Customers and providers contact each other directly and agree on work, price, timing, and payment between themselves.

2. Accounts and account safety

Give accurate account details, keep your password private, and contact customer care if you believe someone accessed your account. You are responsible for activity performed through your account until you report a problem. Automated account recovery is not available in this release.

3. Provider listings and photos

Providers are responsible for their service descriptions, prices, locations, contact details, and photos. Only upload material you have permission to share. Do not post unlawful, deceptive, harmful, discriminatory, or infringing content. SkillLink may hide content that violates these rules.

4. Customer ratings

Signed-in customers may submit one rating per provider and later update it. Ratings are community-submitted and are not proof that a booking, payment, or job was verified by SkillLink. A reviewer's first name and last initial appear with their rating. Do not post false, abusive, threatening, or private personal information in a review.

5. Safety, payments, and disputes

Provider profiles are not currently identity-checked, endorsed, or insured by SkillLink. Confirm qualifications and job details directly. SkillLink does not process payments, provide emergency response, guarantee outcomes, or mediate disputes in this release. Never share your M-Pesa PIN or one-time codes.

6. Acceptable use and availability

Do not misuse the service, attempt unauthorized access, interfere with other users, scrape personal data, or upload malware. We may change or suspend features to maintain or protect the service. We aim to keep SkillLink available but cannot promise uninterrupted access.
