Help & customer care.

Quick answers for customers and providers using SkillLink.

Finding and contacting a provider

How do I find a service?

Search by skill or service, add a town or area, and optionally set a maximum starting price. Open a provider profile to see the details and contact them.

Are provider listings verified?

Not yet. Providers enter their own profile details. Confirm the work, qualifications, location, and price directly with them before agreeing to a job.

How do I save a provider or leave a rating?

Sign in with a customer account. Save a provider from the directory or open their profile to add or update your rating. Ratings are community feedback and are not verified job records. The reviewer's first name and last initial are displayed.

Provider accounts and photos

How do I change my services or profile photos?

Sign in, open your provider dashboard from the menu, edit your profile, then save it. You can set a main profile picture and add up to six work photos to your public gallery.

What if I cannot sign in?

Use the email address you registered with. Use the Forgot your password link on the sign-in page. The reset email link expires after one hour and works once. In the public production site, new accounts must verify their email before signing in.
