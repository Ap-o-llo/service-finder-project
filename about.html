ABOUT SKILLLINK

A good skill deserves
a way to be found.

SkillLink is a simple service directory connecting people who need a job done with the people who know how to do it.

01

For customers

Search across services, places, and starting prices. Open a profile, compare your options, and contact the provider directly.

Explore the directory →
02

For skilled people

Describe your work in your own words, set a starting price, add your service area, and let customers find your listing.

Add your skills →
03

Free at launch

Searching and creating a listing are free in this first version. Payments and M-Pesa integration are planned for a later stage.

BUILDING IN STAGES

Useful first. Better over time.

Now

Shared accounts, provider services, customer favourites, and direct contact.

Next

Account dashboards, service updates, and customer saved lists.

Later

Payment options, reviews, and richer discovery tools.

Current version: profiles and saved providers are stored in the configured SkillLink database and shared between signed-in users.
