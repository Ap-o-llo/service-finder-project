CONTACT WITH CARE

A few good habits
go a long way.

SkillLink helps you find contact details. You and the provider agree on the work, price, and arrangements directly.

Before you agree

Be clear on the job

Discuss the work, materials, call-out costs, timing, and final price before work begins. A starting price may not be the full quote.

Before you meet

Choose a comfortable plan

Confirm who is coming and when. For an unfamiliar provider, consider having someone else present and tell a trusted person about the visit.

Before you pay

Use care with money

Do not share your M-Pesa PIN or one-time codes. Agree on deposits and payment timing directly, and keep a record of what you both agreed.

While browsing

Check the details

Listings are entered by providers and are not currently identity-checked or endorsed by SkillLink. Customer ratings are community-submitted and are not verified job records. Confirm qualifications where the work requires them.

What this version can do

SkillLink uses provider accounts and a shared database, but it does not identity-check or endorse providers, process payments, mediate disputes, or provide emergency support. Customer and provider contact is direct. If you feel unsafe or suspect a crime, contact the relevant local authorities.
